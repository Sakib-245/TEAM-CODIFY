About this event

You’re invited to a vibrant Street Food Carnival filled with flavors, fun, and festivity! Experience a wide variety of delicious street foods from different regions all in one place. From spicy chaats to sweet treats, there’s something to satisfy every craving. Enjoy live cooking stations and watch your favorite dishes being made fresh. The carnival also features music, games, and a lively atmosphere for all age groups. Bring your friends and family to share unforgettable foodie moments together. Discover new tastes and indulge in your all-time favorites. Hygiene and quality are our top priorities for a safe dining experience. Don’t miss this exciting celebration of food and culture. Join us and make your day full of taste, joy, and memories!
